Capabilities

From physics to a simulator you can fly.

We build tailored simulation environments around your vehicle, your aerodynamic data and your engineering objectives.

01 / AERO

Aerodynamic Modelling

CFD-informed aerodynamic databases, coefficient models, interpolation, force and moment reconstruction, and model validation.

02 / 6DOF

Flight Dynamics

Nonlinear 6-DOF equations of motion, atmosphere, wind, propulsion, mass properties, actuators and vehicle-specific physics.

03 / GNC

Control & Allocation

Control-law prototyping, PID and cascaded architectures, actuator allocation, trim analysis and closed-loop evaluation.

04 / SIM

Real-Time Simulation

Interactive simulators with pilot inputs, real-time pacing, telemetry and 3D visualization for engineering and demonstration.

05 / V&V

Model Validation

Simulation-to-flight comparison, parameter tuning, sensitivity studies and progressive model improvement using test data.

06 / SIL · HIL

Integration & Testing

Engineering environments designed to evolve toward SIL and HIL workflows, autopilot integration and control-system testing.

Engineering workflow

One continuous model-development loop.

01 Understand

Vehicle architecture, available data, requirements and simulation objectives.

02 Model

Aerodynamics, propulsion, environment, actuators and equations of motion.

03 Simulate

Trim, open-loop dynamics, closed-loop control and real-time interaction.

04 Validate

Compare against CFD, flight-test or experimental data and iterate.
